In practice, a business user is in the middle of an analysis, and a piece of data is missing: a column, a table, a measure. They have two options. [clic] Either they hack it on their own, with an export and an Excel file. That’s shadow data: what they learned never makes it back into the platform. [clic] Or they open a ticket, and wait for the data team to prepare the data. We used to open a ticket to get a dashboard. Now we open a ticket to get a column in the semantic layer. That’s ticketing 2.0: the data team is still the bottleneck.
