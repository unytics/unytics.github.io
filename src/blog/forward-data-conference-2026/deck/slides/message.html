People say you need a whole village of analysts to make self-service work. I believe that village already exists, in each of your companies: it’s your business teams. So keep conversational AI for those who consume. [clic] But when you get back, ask yourself one question: in your company, who could become a builder tomorrow? Today, our data team no longer builds simple dashboards. It co-builds the operational transformation of the company.
