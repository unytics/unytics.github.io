But we are among data teams here: we know what’s under the hood. Anthropic’s data team told the story very well in June. [clic] Plugged straight into their warehouse, Claude gets 21% of answers right. [clic] With a senior team curating the context — reference tables, semantic layer, skills, tests — they climb to over 95%. [clic] And a month later, without maintenance, they fall back to 65%. Context isn’t prepared once: it has to be maintained. [clic] Some drew a conclusion from it: to make self-service work, you need a whole village of analysts.
