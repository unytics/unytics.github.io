For stores, AI comes in two forms. First, specialised agents, embedded in their applications. For example, a store manager looks at the store’s financial statement, and talks it through with an agent that is an expert in optical retail finance: they compare themselves with similar stores, bring in their local context, and get recommendations. [clic] Second, the same expertise is exposed through an MCP server and skills: stores can query their data from their own Claude or their own ChatGPT. [clic] In both cases, the numbers come from the same semantic layer, and everyone sees only their own data.
