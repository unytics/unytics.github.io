07 · Builders and consumers

Who is this model for?

It makes sense if

A company with complex operations

Of a certain size, with many business functions

A data team that has become the build bottleneck

Many processes still manual, around the ERP

It makes less sense if

A digital-native company

No heavy physical operations

A small data team serving everyone through conversational AI

Few manual processes to transform

Paul Marcombes · Forward Data Conference 2026

39

And let’s be honest: this approach isn’t universal. It fits very well in a company of a certain size, with complex operations, where the data team has become the build bottleneck. In our case, it also serves to transform processes that are still very manual. [clic] In a digital-native company, a small data team serving everyone through conversational AI often does the job very well. And transforming manual processes isn’t really a topic there.
