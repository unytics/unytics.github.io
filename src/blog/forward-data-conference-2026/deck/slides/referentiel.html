Then trust set in, and complexity jumped. Example: the owner of the store master data, in Salesforce. Stores change all the time, with acquisitions and openings. Before, we were the ones writing dbt tests on this master data. When a test failed, we let him know, and he fixed it after the fact. [clic] Today, he has built his own cockpit, far more detailed than our tests. For example, when a store is taken over, the opening dates of the old and the new owner must not overlap. He sees the inconsistencies, and he can be proactive. He is the one who knows these business rules. Not us.
