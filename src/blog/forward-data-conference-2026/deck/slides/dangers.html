Two dangers remain: drift and review

4 reviewers for 25 contributors and their agents

The bottleneck moves: from tickets to pull requests.

Paul Marcombes · Forward Data Conference 2026

30

Two real dangers lurk. The first is drift: as soon as the same thing is described in several places, the versions end up diverging, and the AI follows the wrong one. [clic] The second is review. Four people can’t review everything that twenty-five contributors and their agents produce. If we’re not careful, we have just moved the bottleneck, from tickets to pull requests.
