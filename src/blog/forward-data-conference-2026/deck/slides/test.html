When it left MicroStrategy, Optic 2000 first chose Tableau. And the BI team spent a phenomenal amount of time on it, on repetitive, tedious point-and-click tasks. We offered them a test: build their dashboards as Streamlit applications, by vibe-coding in our repo, with AI. They were sceptical themselves: will it be clean? Will the numbers be right? Will we be able to maintain it? [clic] Within a few weeks, the shift was spectacular: they became autonomous. [clic] The end-user experience is ten times better, for a fraction of the time we used to spend on Tableau. [clic] And above all, they didn’t stop at Streamlit: on their own, they started creating and maintaining their own dbt models.
