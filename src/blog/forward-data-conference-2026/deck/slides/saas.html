The first is replacing SaaS tools. In geomarketing, we paid for a rather rigid proprietary tool to visualise catchment and franchise areas on the map of France. The person in charge rebuilt the whole tool in Streamlit. It finally meets 100% of her needs, and we drop a costly license. [clic] Same for the stores’ tax returns. Instead of re-entering them by hand every year, or paying a lot for OCR software, we have an automatic recognition app, with a human in the loop to validate. With exactly the same quality.
