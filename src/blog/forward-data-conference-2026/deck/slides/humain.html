The second: the key isn’t technology, it’s people. Of our four data engineers, one works full time onboarding and coaching business users. [clic] And our BI team’s job is changing. Freed from ticketing and point-and-click, it is becoming proactive. Often, business teams don’t just need one more number: they need an outside view to rethink the way they work. And in our case, the job is huge: it’s on this platform that the BI team will help transform all those processes that are still manual.
